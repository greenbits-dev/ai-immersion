# Homework after Session 4

*Your site is live. The only assignment now, and every week after: keep it alive.*

## 1. Ship one improvement (~30–60 min)

One full loop, alone, on your live site:

1. Fresh Claude Code session in your site's folder
2. Plan: "I want to add/change X. Propose a plan, don't build yet."
3. Approve → build → **verify on your phone** → commit & push
4. Watch it go live
5. Add a line to your log.md

Ideas if stuck: a projects section, a photo, a /now page, better wording, a favicon. Ask Claude: *"suggest 5 small improvements, ranked by effort."*

## 2. Send the URL to one person

Not for feedback theatrics — because a thing with an audience gets maintained, and a thing without one gets abandoned.

## 3. Re-take the ladder

Re-read the [competence ladder](../01-big-picture.md) and honestly place yourself again. Bring your before/after levels to session 5.

## 4. Bring your real project idea

Session 5 plans YOUR project (from the session-2 homework) plus skills, MCP, and what to explore next.
