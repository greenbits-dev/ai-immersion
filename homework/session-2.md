# Homework after Session 2

*Your workshop is installed. This week is about getting comfortable in it.*

## 1. Talk to Claude Code daily (~10 min/day)

Open the course repo folder, start `claude`, and each day try one of these:

- "Look around this folder and explain how it's organized."
- "Quiz me on lesson 2 — one question at a time."
- "Show me what `git status` does here and explain the output."
- "What does the CLAUDE.md in this repo tell you to do?"
- "Explain tokens to me using a different analogy than the course uses."

Goal: the terminal stops feeling scary. Nothing you type in conversation can break anything.

## 2. Read

- [Lesson 4 — The workflow](../04-workflow.md) and [Lesson 5 — Principles](../05-principles.md), before session 3.

## 3. Prepare your project idea

Refine your "what would I build if building were easy" line into 3–5 sentences: what the thing is, who it's for, what's on it. Session 4 builds your personal site first — but your idea is the follow-up project, and we'll plan it in session 5.
